# 👤 About Me — Shehram Khan

I am an **Industrial & Process Control Engineer** and **Data Scientist** specializing in predictive maintenance, manufacturing quality robustness, applied machine learning, and production generative AI systems.

With a foundation in **Industrial Engineering (B.S.)** and **Engineering Management (M.S.)** from the University of Windsor alongside a **Master of Science in Data Science** from Bellevue University, I focus on solving high-stakes operational and business problems where physical manufacturing meets advanced analytics.

### 🎯 Engineering & Data Science Philosophy
* **Validation Hygiene & Zero Data Leakage:** Every predictive model is strictly partitioned prior to scaling, imputation, and feature synthesis to ensure real-world generalization.
* **Domain-Constrained Safety Guardrails:** Whether deploying error-proofing on automotive assembly lines or fine-tuning clinical AI workout engines, hard physical and biomechanical safety rules are enforced programmatically.
* **Quantifiable Business & Operational ROI:** Models are benchmarked on economic cost savings, downtime reduction, and decision-threshold precision rather than vanity metrics alone.

### 🛠️ Core Technical Stack
* **Programming & Databases:** Python, R, SQL, VBA, MATLAB
* **Machine Learning & Deep Learning:** XGBoost, LightGBM, `scikit-learn`, TensorFlow / Keras (CNN), Holt-Winters, PCA, `GridSearchCV`
* **Generative AI & LLM Systems:** OpenAI API, Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), Structured Outputs (`json_object`), Schema Grounding
* **Deployment & Visualization:** Streamlit, MLflow, Power BI, Matplotlib, Seaborn, `python-pptx`, GitHub Pages
* **Industrial & Quality Systems:** Predictive Telemetry, Control Plans, PFMEA, Poka-Yoke Error-Proofing
