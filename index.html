About Me

I am an Engineer and Data Scientist specializing in predictive maintenance, process robustness, statistical quality control, and end-to-end machine learning pipelines. With a foundation in Industrial Engineering (B.S.) and Engineering Management (M.E.M.) from the University of Windsor alongside my Master of Science in Data Science at Bellevue University, I focus on solving high-stakes operational and business problems where physical manufacturing meets advanced analytics.

Whether architecting leak-free telemetry pipelines that prevent catastrophic tooling failures on automotive assembly lines, compressing high-dimensional regression spaces with PCA, or automating executive data storytelling, my approach prioritizes model interpretability, strict validation hygiene, and quantifiable economic impact.
