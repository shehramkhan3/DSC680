Background & Philosophy

About Me

I am an Industrial & Process Control Engineer and Data Scientist specializing in predictive maintenance, manufacturing quality robustness, applied machine learning, and production generative AI systems. Holding a Bachelor of Science in Industrial Engineering and a Master of Science in Engineering Management from the University of Windsor alongside my Master of Science in Data Science at Bellevue University, I bridge physical manufacturing operations with modern statistical and deep learning architectures.

Across automotive assembly and high-volume production environments, I have architected smart tooling error-proofing implementations, PFMEAs, Control Plans, and real-time sensor telemetry pipelines that prevent defects before they reach downstream stations.

In my data science practice, I apply that exact same engineering discipline—leak-free validation hygiene, domain-constrained safety guardrails, model interpretability, and quantifiable financial ROI—whether reducing simulated plant downtime by $2.37M with XGBoost or deploying a supervised fine-tuned clinical AI workout engine in Streamlit.
