Industrial ML & Telemetry $2.37M Net Savings

Predictive Maintenance Telemetry Pipeline

Engineered an end-to-end industrial ML pipeline across 10,000 sensor observations (temperature, rotational speed, torque) to detect catastrophic equipment failures before breakdown.

  • Methodology: Quarantined leakage flags, standardized on training partitions, and engineered physics-informed features (Power Index & Mechanical Strain Index).
  • Outcome: XGBoost achieved 98.60% accuracy and 86.8% failure recall, cutting simulated downtime costs by 77.4% ($2,368,500) over 2,000 production cycles.

Computer Vision / Deep Learning 98.89% Test Accuracy

MNIST Handwritten Digit Classification CNN

Deployed a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) to classify handwritten digits, demonstrating the spatial feature extraction advantage of Conv2D filters over dense architectures.

  • Methodology: Normalized grayscale image tensors and built two Conv2D/MaxPooling2D blocks feeding a 10-node softmax classifier with Adam optimization.
  • Outcome: Converged in 5 epochs to 98.89% accuracy (0.0318 test loss); Seaborn confusion matrix error analysis isolated ambiguous digit pairs (4 vs. 9, 3 vs. 8).

Dimensionality Reduction & Regression R² 0.65 → 0.84

Housing Price Regression & Dimensionality Reduction

Optimized a high-dimensional housing price regression model by benchmarking feature pruning, Variance Thresholding, and Principal Component Analysis (PCA).

  • Methodology: Filtered columns with greater than 40% missingness, imputed medians/modes across 236 dummy variables, and applied PCA to retain 90% explained variance.
  • Outcome: Compressed feature space to 127 components, boosting R² from 0.6478 to 0.8409 and cutting RMSE from 51,973 to 34,938.

U.S. Retail Sales Time-Series Forecasting

Modeled macroeconomic U.S. retail sales trends and 12-month seasonality across three decades of historical census data (1992–2021).

  • Methodology: Reshaped wide-format matrices into a continuous monthly-start DatetimeIndex, analyzing structural shocks (2008 Recession, 2020 COVID-19).
  • Outcome: Trained an Additive Holt-Winters Exponential Smoothing model on 342 months, achieving a 5.69% MAPE across an unseen 12-month test horizon.
